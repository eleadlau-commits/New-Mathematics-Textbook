# Hand-crafted construction

E rebuilds the blur structure by hand, from the first line, as E learns it.

- Every line in this folder is written or chosen by E, in E's words.
- Claude guides, one small step at a time, and offers phrasing only when asked.
- Nothing here is copied from the book (`rules.tex`, the buildings). The book stays as reference.

## How E works

E writes in a plain editable doc ("Chapter 0, by hand"). When a chapter settles, it is turned into TeX here.

## Files

- `DECISIONS.md`: what E has decided, in order, with the date.
- `00-start.md`: the construction itself, growing line by line.
- `ch0-working.tex` + `preamble.tex`: E's working copy of Chapter 0, started as an exact copy of `rules.tex`. Upload both to Overleaf and compile `ch0-working.tex`. E edits this copy; `rules.tex` stays as it is.
